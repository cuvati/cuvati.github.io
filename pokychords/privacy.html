PokyChords — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-23 · Last updated: 2026-06-23

PokyChords ("the app", "we", "us") is a local-first songbook app operated by Remy Chu. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not. We designed PokyChords to keep your data on your device.

The short version

  • Your songs stay on your device. There is no account, no login, and no PokyChords server that stores your songs, lyrics, chords, or library.
  • The app connects to the internet in only six situations, all described below: (1) importing a song from a web URL, (2) browsing the web in the in-app Find browser, (3) the optional "Normalize with AI" / "Reformat with AI" actions, (4) the optional "Suggest similar songs" action, (5) playing an attached YouTube video, and (6) an abuse-prevention rate limit tied to the AI actions.
  • The in-app browser can reach any website you navigate to. We do not restrict it to chord-sheet sites, and we do not record where you go.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, track you across apps or websites, or run analytics.

Data stored on your device

Songs you import or create — including titles, artists, lyrics, chords, keys, capo, tags, favorites, and transpose settings — are stored locally on your device only. They are not transmitted to us or backed up to our servers. If you delete a song in the app or uninstall the app, that data is removed from your device. We have no copy.

You can export your whole songbook to a backup file yourself (Settings → Back up songbook) and restore it later. That file is written by your device and handed to whichever app you choose to save or send it with — it never passes through us. Where it then lives, and who can read it, is up to you.

When the app connects to the internet

The rest of the app — opening, searching, editing, transposing, favoriting, and viewing songs — works fully offline. The app contacts the network only here:

1. Importing a song from a web URL

When you share or paste a link to a supported chord-sheet website, the app fetches that page directly from the third-party website, on your device, to read the chords and lyrics. That request goes to the website you chose (not to PokyChords), and that website receives it like any normal web request (including your device's IP address and request headers). We do not control and are not responsible for those third-party websites; their own privacy practices apply. PokyChords does not send the imported page or the resulting song to us.

2. Finding songs on the web (the in-app browser)

Find opens a normal web search inside the app so you can see a page before importing it. From there you can follow links like you would in any browser — so the app can load any website you navigate to. Those pages load directly from the sites themselves; PokyChords does not proxy, log, or receive them, and we do not record your browsing. Each site you visit sees the request like any normal web request (including your device's IP address and request headers), and its own privacy practices and cookies apply. Only the page you explicitly choose to import is read for chords, and that reading happens on your device.

3. Optional "Normalize with AI" / "Reformat with AI"

AI formatting only ever runs when you tap a button — never automatically. Two cases: if an import can't be read automatically, the app saves it as an editable draft and you may tap "Normalize with AI"; or, on a song you've already saved, you may tap "Reformat with AI" to tidy its formatting. In both cases the app sends only that song's chord/lyric text to our backend (a serverless function), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API and returns a tidy song sheet.

  • This happens only on your explicit tap — never automatically, and never during normal use (capture, search, viewing, editing, or transposing).
  • The submitted text is processed transiently to produce the result. We do not store your lyrics, chords, titles, artists, or the normalized output on our servers, and we do not write them to our logs (we log only technical measures such as text length and token counts).
  • The text is processed by Google as our service provider. Your use of this feature is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

4. Optional "Suggest similar songs"

Only when you tap "Suggest similar songs", the app sends the titles, artists, and categories of a few of your favorite songs to our backend, which forwards them to Google's Gemini API and returns a short list of suggested song titles with a one-line reason each.

  • No lyrics or chords are ever sent for this feature — titles, artists, and categories only.
  • Same policy as above: the submitted text is processed transiently, we do not store it or the returned suggestions on our servers, and we do not write any of it to our logs (we log only technical measures such as item counts, byte sizes, and token counts).
  • Tapping a suggestion just runs a normal web search for that song's chords; you see real web results before anything is imported.
  • The text is processed by Google as our service provider, subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

5. Play-along video (YouTube)

If you attach a YouTube video to a song, the play-along dock loads that video from YouTube inside the app, and searching for one loads YouTube's mobile search page. That content comes from Google/YouTube directly, and Google receives it like any normal YouTube view — including your device's IP address, and Google's own cookies and terms. This is the one part of a song sheet that is not offline: the chords and lyrics always render offline, the video never does. PokyChords stores only the 11-character video id with the song, on your device.

6. Abuse-prevention rate limit

Because the AI features have no login, we apply a simple per-source rate limit to prevent abuse and control cost. When you use "Normalize with AI", "Reformat with AI", or "Suggest similar songs", our backend records, for that request only:

  • a salted, one-way hash of your IP address (not your IP address itself, and not reversible to it in normal use),
  • a time window, and
  • a request count.

This record contains no song content and is not linked to any account or identity (there is none). It exists solely to count recent requests per source and is used only for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or logins.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage telemetry.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No crash-reporting collection is active in this version.

Third-party services

  • Supabase hosts the serverless function and the rate-limit record described above (operational data only — no song content).
  • Google Gemini API processes the text you submit only when you use "Normalize with AI", to return a normalized song sheet.

These providers process data on our behalf for the limited purposes above. We do not authorize them to use your submitted text for their own purposes.

Data retention

  • On-device songs: kept until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • AI-submitted text: not retained by us after the request is processed.
  • Rate-limit records: operational metadata only (hashed source + counters), retained briefly for the rate-limiting window and periodically cleaned up.

Children's privacy

PokyChords is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the in-app Find browser can reach any website, the app is rated for older users on the App Store and is not suitable for young children.

Your choices

  • Everything except importing, Find, the AI actions, and video playback works offline; you can use the app without ever triggering a network request.
  • You control whether "Normalize with AI", "Reformat with AI", and "Suggest similar songs" are ever used — each requires your tap.
  • Attaching a YouTube video to a song is optional; a song with no video attached never contacts YouTube.
  • You can delete any song in the app, and uninstalling removes all on-device data. Because we hold no account and no copy of your library, there is no server-side account to delete.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect the limited data described here (for example, HTTPS for network requests and a salted hash for the rate-limit record). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the data we touch deliberately minimal.

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice within the app or store listing as appropriate.
